CSV to Numbers

Opens CSV files in Apple Numbers without Numbers changing your SKUs. 00123 stays 00123, and 1234500 keeps its zeros.

You need a Mac with Numbers. Nothing else to install.

Install (2 minutes, one time only)

  1. Unzip CSV-to-Numbers.zip if you haven't already (double-click it). You'll get a folder with the app and this guide.
  2. Drag CSV to Numbers into your Applications folder.
  3. Open CSV to Numbers from Applications.
  4. macOS will warn that it "could not verify" the app. This is expected, because the app isn't from the App Store. Click Done, then:
    1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    2. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to "CSV to Numbers was blocked".
    3. Enter your Mac password and click Open.
  5. A small window appears confirming it's ready. Click Done.
If Open Anyway doesn't appear, see Troubleshooting on the last page.

Use

Pick whichever you prefer:

  • Right-click a CSV in Finder → Quick Actions → Open in Numbers (as text).
  • Drag CSV files onto the app.
  • Double-click a CSV, once you've made this app the default (see below).

The file opens in Numbers with every cell as text. Your original CSV isn't changed, and no extra files are left behind.

Make double-click work for CSV files (optional)

  1. Click any .csv file once and press ⌘ + I.
  2. Under Open with, choose CSV to Numbers.
  3. Click Change All… and confirm.

To undo this, repeat the steps and choose another app.

Things to know

  • Everything opens as text. To add up a price or quantity column, select it and change its format to Number. Leave the SKU column as Text.
  • The file opens with Numbers' standard table styling, not your own default template. To use your template, copy the table into a new document. Copying from Numbers to Numbers keeps the text as it is.
  • Files saved with commas, semicolons or tabs all work, as do Excel-style special characters.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Open Anyway isn't in System SettingsOpen Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/CSV to Numbers.app", then open the app again.
No right-click optionOpen CSV to Numbers once from Applications. If it's still missing, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Extensions → Finder and enable Open in Numbers (as text).
The right-click option does nothingThe app has been moved or deleted. Put CSV to Numbers back in Applications.
Zeros are still being droppedCheck you opened the file with this app or the right-click option, not by double-clicking while Numbers is the default.

Uninstall

Drag CSV to Numbers from Applications to the Bin, then delete ~/Library/Services/Open in Numbers (as text).workflow.
